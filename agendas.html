Conference decisions

Three events, one budget
All three are competing for the same money, and FocusedFit is working to a limited budget. Prices below are verified from each organiser's own registration page on 12 October. The free applications are worth doing regardless, because they change what any ticket needs to buy.
15 to 18 November 2026 · Five weeks away

HLTH USA 2026

Venetian ExpoLas Vegas12,250+ attendees

The biggest of the three, and where FocusedFit's buyers are. Christel dislikes the venue and comes home ill, but called it worth attending.

  1. Startup rate: $1,500, and FocusedFit qualifiesAgainst a full rate of $3,395. The test is founded under 4 years ago, under $8M raised, actively building a product. FocusedFit was founded January 2025 with no funding raised, so it clears both comfortably. Limited to two attendees per company, so $3,000 for Christel and Chris.
  2. If FocusedFit does not qualify, the rate is $3,695That is the Solution Providers and Vendors rate, which is what Alvarez Search would pay. The startup rate explicitly excludes consulting firms, service providers and advisors, which is why this has to go under FocusedFit.
  3. Market Connect is free, and closes 2 NovemberWorth $3,395. For senior people actively evaluating or buying health tech. Eight curated meetings, plus a $750 travel credit for completing twelve or more. Christel may not qualify as a buyer, but it costs one application to find out.
  4. The pitch competition is closedApplications shut on 26 June. It reopens for HLTH USA 2027, which runs 17 to 20 October 2027 in Las Vegas.
Verification still matters. Every rate except Solution Providers is checked, and if the organisation does not qualify HLTH gives three business days to pay the difference or cancel with a $150 fee. The eligibility is sound, but the exclusion covers consulting firms and service providers, so the registration has to read as FocusedFit: product company email, product title. Chris is the safer name on it if either registration gets queried.
14 to 17 March 2027 · Five months away

ViVE 2027

NashvilleSame organiser as HLTHDigital health focus

The sister event to HLTH, aimed squarely at digital health decision makers and the business of healthcare. Cheapest of the three, and in a city Christel already has reasons to visit.

  1. Startup rate: $1,395, rising to $1,595Identical criteria to HLTH, and FocusedFit qualifies on both. By March 2027 it will be about 2 years 2 months old, still well inside the 4 year limit. Two per company, so $2,790 at the current rate.
  2. Solution Providers: $1,995, against a full rate of $2,995Worth knowing, because it is half what HLTH charges the same category. If FocusedFit fails the startup test, ViVE is still affordable and HLTH is not.
  3. Provider and Payer Connect is freeWorth $2,395, with the same eight meetings and $750 travel credit structure.
  4. The 2027 pitch competition is not yet publishedThe page still shows 2026 content. Criteria last year were under 5 years old and under $8M raised. Worth watching for the application to open.
Why this may be the strongest of the three. Nashville rather than Las Vegas, cheapest entry, digital health rather than general health, and March gives the free trial time to exist before they walk in.

Noah Liff Opera CenterNashvilleCapped at 250

Phase Capital's fourth annual summit. Small, curated, and the only one of the three with a no pitching and no selling rule.

The caveat: it leans biotech and life sciences more than health IT. Speakers come from Xontogeny, Transcripta Bio, RA Capital and Form Bio. Worth reading the 2026 white paper before committing.
